Picture settings

Picture

The picture to OBS is always landscape. Hold the phone sideways with the iPhone rotation lock off (Control Center) for the full picture.

Lens

Tap 24mm to 200mm at the bottom of the screen, or pinch to zoom in between. On phones with a zoom lens (iPhone Pro), the long settings switch to that lens by themselves so the picture stays sharp; without one they are a digital zoom.

White balance

Kelvin

This phone's browser does not allow white balance control. It stays on auto.

Exposure

Brightness: drag the ☀ line on the right of the screen up or down. Double-tap it to reset.

Shutter
ISO

Under tube lights, set the shutter to 1/50 or 1/100 to stop flicker (Nigeria mains is 50 Hz).

This phone's browser does not allow exposure control. It stays on auto.
