Settings

Open on your phone

The first link stays the same even when this computer's number changes. If it does not open on a phone (some Android phones), use the number link.

Phone and this computer must be on the same Wi-Fi. If Windows asks about the firewall for OBS, allow Private networks.

Phones lose Switch until you open the GloryCast Switch app again.

OBS link

In OBS: Tools > WebSocket Server Settings > Enable, then Show Connect Info for the password.

Stream

Switch makes a scene called GloryCast Program in OBS with your channel cameras inside it, and switches cameras within it. Add your logo, Bible and hymn display and lower thirds at the top of that scene once; they stay on whichever camera is live. Fades work by themselves: pick Fade and a length (up to 20 s).

OBS streams GloryCast On Air (it holds GloryCast Program) with Studio Mode on, so you can click any scene in OBS and add things to it in Preview while the service is live. Leave OBS's Transition button alone; the phone does the switching.

Camera channels

Across × down. 4×2 is 8 channels.

Arrange: tap a channel, pick its camera (or Empty). You can also hold any channel to start.

Layout

Or drag the little bar between Preview / Live and the cameras.
